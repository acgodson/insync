“I don't know that sign yet”

Almero says when it's unsure, instead of guessing. Teach it a client's name or a work word: record it twice, test it, save it. Then sign it again, and it's understood.

Alongside human interpreters, not instead of them

Book a qualified interpreter for interviews, medical, legal and big meetings. Almero is for the everyday calls that often happen with no support at all.

Where it stands

A hackathon prototype. The avatar signs one sign at a time, without facial grammar, so it isn't full BSL. Deaf users haven't tested it yet, and that comes next.
