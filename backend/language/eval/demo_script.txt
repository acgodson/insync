# The hackathon demo conversation, scripted. Classroom: Bob is the teacher, Alice the Deaf student.
# Milestone 3: "hello" both directions
say Hello Alice!
sign HELLO
wait 2
# Bob asks; Alice answers with an ambiguous middle sign
say What is your name?
sign I:.93
sign NAME:.71 WATER:.62
end
# Reply direction with synonyms and a name
say Good. Please open your textbook. Chidi will help you.
# Alice doesn't understand; recogniser unsure between UNDERSTAND and KNOW
sign I:.9
sign NO:.88
sign UNDERSTAND:.66 KNOW:.61
end
say Okay, I will repeat it slowly.
# Emergency: spoken immediately, no pause wait
sign FIRE:.91
# Teach it live: an unknown sign, taught, then usable in both directions
sign SCIENCE:.31
teach SCIENCE science
say Today we learn science.
sign SCIENCE:.89
sign GOOD:.85
end
